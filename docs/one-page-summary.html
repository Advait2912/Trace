TRACE: Memory in Motion

In-Context Learning with Recurrent Memory
DataForge 2026 × Pathway Track • Problem Statement 1 Submission • Dual-Engine Parity Verified
The Central Falsifiable Claim: A fixed-size recurrent state can absorb an unbounded number of key–value demonstrations without growing in size, but when two demonstrations share overlapping (non-orthogonal) keys, the readout for one leaks the value of the other — a measurable, reproducible form of forgetting caused by fixed capacity, not a scripted failure. In our controlled experiment, increasing the state dimension allows construction of more mutually orthogonal keys and therefore reduces the interference we observe.

1. The Computational Substrate

Standard Transformer LLMs require expanding O(T) KV caches to remember context. TRACE instantiates recurrent in-context learning where memory is an evolving matrix of invariant size:

  • Recurrent State: S ∈ ℝd×d, initialized to S0 = 0d×d (invariant d2 scalars, independent of sequence length T).
  • Demonstration Update: St = St-1 + vt ktT (rank-1 outer product write; standard fast-weight convention).
  • Normalized Readout: ŷ = (St · kq) / (kq · kq).
  • Derived Leakage: ŷa = va + ∑b≠a [(ka · kb) / (ka · ka)] vb. Leakage is analytically proven by linear algebra, not simulated by arbitrary noise or scripted failure.

2. Key Experimental Findings (Frozen Fixtures)

  1. Lossless Recall (d=3): Absorbing red→1, blue→2, green→3 into S3 ∈ ℝ3×3. With orthonormal keys (ki · kj = 0), querying green returns 3.000 with 0.000 error.
  2. Interference from Overlapping Keys (d=2): Demonstrating kA → 1.0 and kB → 9.0 with kA · kB = 0.60. Querying kA yields 6.400 (1.0 + 0.6 × 9.0), matching analytical leak coefficient 0.60 to machine precision.
  3. Capacity Threshold (d ∈ [2, 6]): For N=4 demonstrations, MSE is substantial at d=2 (3.13), but drops to exact machine precision (1.26 × 10-31) at d ≥ 4 where orthogonal basis allocation becomes possible.

4. Literature Grounding & Honest Scope Boundary

Primary Papers (2022–2026):

  • BDH-CQ (2026): Engdahl, Kosowski, Chorowski, Stamirowska et al., arXiv:2608.09888. §3.2 defines general update St = Uθ(St-1, Dt) and named additive special case St = St-1 + Uθ(Dt).
  • The Dragon Hatchling (2025): Kosowski, Uznański, Chorowski, Stamirowska, & Bartoszkiewicz, arXiv:2509.26507. Biological foundations of fast synaptic state.
  • Mamba (2023): Gu & Dao, arXiv:2312.00752. Theoretical analysis of fixed-capacity recurrent state and compression limits.

Foundational Mechanism (Pre-2022): Schlag et al. (2021) & Katharopoulos et al. (2020) — linear attention as fast-weight associative memory.

Explicit Non-Claim: TRACE is an educational toy model instantiating the additive special case named in BDH-CQ §3.2. It does not reconstruct BDH-CQ's proprietary, trained Uθ function, sparsity profile (~5% active), or ARC-AGI model weights.
